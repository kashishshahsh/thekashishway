how can a brand that shaped our childhood remain relevant to a generation reshaping itself?

The strongest brands aren't remembered because they stay the same.
They're remembered because they evolve without losing who they are.

Challenge

Oreo is one of the most recognisable snack brands in the world. As younger consumers become increasingly health-conscious, this explores how the brand could extend into a new category without losing its core identity.

Strategy

Leveraging Oreo's existing emotional connection with consumers and translate it into a new category. The concept balances familiarity and aspiration, allowing the brand to enter the fitness space without losing its recognisable identity.
