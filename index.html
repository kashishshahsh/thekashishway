kashish shah

i believe the most meaningful design already exists around us.

it lives in crafts, traditions, rituals, museums, streets, and everyday objects. these systems weren't created to decorate life — they were created to make life simpler, more meaningful, and more connected.

my work is about studying those systems and reimagining them for the present.
